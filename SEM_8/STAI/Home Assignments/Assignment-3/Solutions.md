### Q1: Mention two pioneering products introduced by CSIR that had a significant impact on India's food and agricultural sectors.

**Answer:** The Madras Observatory. It was originally founded as a private facility by William Petrie in 1786 and officially established by the East India Company in 1790 to systematize astronomical and meteorological data collection.
